Under the hood

Why this is hard, and why it matters

One model

A single multilingual model trained across Nigerian English, Nigerian Pidgin, Yoruba, Igbo and Hausa. It speaks all five and switches between them mid-conversation without changing person.

Accent, disentangled

Most systems fuse accent and language, so a voice either loses its identity abroad or snaps to a newsreader accent. Ours separates who is speaking from what is being spoken. That separation comes from the data, not the architecture.

Accent is a dial

Preserve the speaker's accent for authenticity, or neutralise it towards a native target when the use case calls for it. A bank line and a radio drama want different settings. Both are one parameter.
